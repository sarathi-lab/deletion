Account & Data Deletion Request

App Name: NMCLE Quiz App

Developer: Sarathi Labs

How to Request Deletion

In-App Account Deletion: Any user logged into an account can permanently delete their account and associated data directly within the app:

  1. Tap the Profile icon in the top right corner.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the profile dialog and tap “Delete Account & Data”.
  3. Confirm the permanent deletion prompt.

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data:

  1. Send an email to sarathi.quiz@gmail.com from the email address linked to your app account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered email address or User ID in the body of the message.

Requests are processed manually within 7 business days.

Data Handled Upon Request

  • Data Deleted: Your Firebase Authentication credentials, profile information, and all user records stored in our database.
  • Data Retained: None. All user data is permanently removed.
  • Retention Period: Any system backups containing your data are automatically purged within 30 days.
